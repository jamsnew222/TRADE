## Full description

```
TradeQuest turns trading education into a game. Learn how markets actually
work through short interactive lessons, then practice with real stock prices
— no deposits, no risk. Sign in once and your progress, streaks, and premium
follow you across every device.

📚 LEARN FIRST, THEN GET TESTED
Every lesson teaches the concept before quizzing you: bite-sized study
cards, key terms, then a mix of question types — multiple choice, true/false,
ordering, matching, fill-in-the-blank, word bank, and real-world scenarios.
From "What is a stock?" to candlestick patterns and day-trading strategies.

📈 TRADE REAL MARKETS — SAFELY
Live Market mode uses real stock prices and real exchange hours across US,
European, and Asian exchanges. Practice 24/7 mode is always open with
simulated prices anchored to real quotes. Your portfolio persists between
sessions, so your practice compounds like the real thing.

🔥 STAY CONSISTENT
Daily streaks with milestone bonuses, streak freezes for busy days, and 3
fresh quests every day that pay bonus XP.

🏆 LEVEL UP
Earn XP for every correct answer, unlock achievements, and climb levels as
your knowledge grows.

💎 TRADEQUEST PREMIUM (optional)
2x XP, unlimited daily usage, premium lessons, unlimited streak freezes,
exclusive badges, and monthly bonus XP. Free tier includes 5 lessons and 25
trades every day — forever, no ads.

EDUCATION ONLY: TradeQuest is a learning tool. Simulated and delayed prices
are for practice, not investment advice.
```

## Play Console — Data safety form (required)
The app now has accounts, so declare:
- **Email address** → collected, required, for account management; stored in
  Firebase; users can delete it in-app (Profile → Delete account)
- **App activity / app progress** → collected for cloud sync, tied to account
- Financial info: NO (no real money). Precise location: NO. Contacts/photos: NO.
- "Is data encrypted in transit?" → YES (HTTPS)
- "Can users request deletion?" → YES, in-app + via store support channel
